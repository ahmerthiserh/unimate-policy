Privacy Policy for UniMate

Last Updated: October 2026

UniMate ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Vetris Tech, is designed as a student utility companion to track courses, assignments, and budgets.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you sign up, we collect your email address and authentication credentials. If you browse as a guest, a temporary anonymous identifier is assigned.
  • Student & Planner Data: Courses, assignment deadlines, and completion statuses you create.
  • Budget Records: Spending categories, expense logs, and amounts entered locally.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data strictly to sync your assignments and budget ledger across your devices and maintain offline persistence via our encrypted database.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your cloud data is stored securely in PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcement. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

4. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the app via Menu > Account > Delete Account, or submit an external deletion request at our Account Deletion Page.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: support@vetristech.com
