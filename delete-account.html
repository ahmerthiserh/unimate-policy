UniMate Account & Data Deletion

Under Google Play user data safety policies, you have full control over your personal data. You can delete your UniMate account and all associated records permanently at any time.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the UniMate app.
  2. Go to the Hub / Menu tab.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your profile, registered courses, planner tasks, and spending records are permanently purged from our database immediately.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have the app installed, send a request from the email linked to your UniMate account:

Send Deletion Request

Requests submitted by email are verified and wiped within 7 business days.

Types of Data Deleted

  • Account credentials and user identifier (UUID).
  • Course data and schedule items.
  • All assignment deadlines and task logs.
  • All financial expense records and budget calculations.
